Tips

Easy ways to care for your energy

Free things you can do on your own. No special tools.

Ask your guides

Say in your head, "Can I have some help?" You don't need to hear an answer. Notice any thoughts, pictures, or feelings. Or notice nothing. Both are okay.

Move

Walk, stretch, dance, or run. Whatever feels good today.

Lead with your heart

When your head gets loud, put a hand on your chest and breathe slowly. Ask, "What does my heart want?"

Eat by feel

Notice how different foods feel in your body. Choose what feels good to you.

Go outside

Spend a little time with trees, sky, and fresh air.

Ground yourself

Stand barefoot on grass or soil, or rest a hand on a tree.

These are ideas, not medical advice.
